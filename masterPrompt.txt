============================================================
FEATURE GROUP 66
SECURITY LIMITATIONS
============================================================

The application must explicitly explain:

A passive scanner cannot determine whether an AP is malicious with certainty.

An unfamiliar SSID is not automatically malicious.

A new BSSID is not automatically an attack.

A security configuration change does not prove compromise.

Signal changes do not prove movement or attack activity.

Channel changes do not prove interference.

Risk scores are heuristic.

Anomalies require investigation.
